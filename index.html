Support

Star Watch is a sky map for iPhone and iPad. If something does not work or you have a suggestion, write to tseng71tseng71@gmail.com. Please mention your device and iOS version.

The map points the wrong way

Move the phone in a figure of eight a few times to recalibrate the compass, and keep it away from magnets and metal cases. Allowing location access lets the app use true north.

The sky does not match where I am

Open Settings in the app and check the location. You can let the app use your current location or enter a latitude and longitude yourself.

Purchases

Every feature is free for your first 10 sessions, with nothing to sign up for. After that a single purchase unlocks everything for good; there is no subscription. If you reinstall or change device, tap Restore Purchases on the purchase screen. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Satellites are missing

Satellite positions need orbital data that the app downloads from time to time. Connect to the internet once and they will appear.

サポート

Star Watch は iPhone・iPad 用の星図アプリです。不具合やご要望は tseng71tseng71@gmail.com までお知らせください。お使いの機種と iOS のバージョンを添えていただけると助かります。

星図の向きがずれる

端末を「8」の字に数回動かしてコンパスを調整し、磁石や金属製ケースから離してください。位置情報を許可すると真北が使われます。

星空が現在地と合わない

アプリ内の「設定」で位置を確認してください。現在地を使うことも、緯度・経度を手入力することもできます。
